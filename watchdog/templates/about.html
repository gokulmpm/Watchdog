Leadership & Research

Founded by Deepak Chowdhary, a veteran foundryman with over 30 years of experience in sand control optimisation and casting rejection reduction. The platform is developed by MPM Infosoft Pvt. Ltd., based in Nagpur, with technical support from IIT Madras Research Park, Chennai.

A foundry processing 2,000 tons/month loses ₹12–15 million annually from just 1% molding sand rejection. SandMan® AI Watchdog directly addresses this problem through real-time parameter monitoring, AI-driven alerts, and prescription compliance tracking.
