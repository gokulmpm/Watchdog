Privacy Policy

MPM Infosoft Pvt. Ltd.  ·  Effective 2026  ·  Contact: legal@mpminfosoft.com

Scope

This policy outlines how MPM Infosoft Pvt. Ltd. ("MPM") manages personal information from users of its products and services. The policy applies only to MPM operations, not subsidiaries or partner companies.

Data Collection

We gather information during registration, product use, and website visits. This includes name, email address, postal code, occupation, industry, and personal interests. We also collect technical data such as IP addresses and browser information.

How We Use Your Data

MPM uses collected information to improve products, conduct statistical analysis, and develop new services. Data is processed on an 'ANONYMOUS' basis for algorithm development and trend analysis.

Data Sharing

MPM does not sell data to third parties. Information may be shared only in the following scenarios:

  • Business mergers or acquisitions
  • Partner service providers under confidentiality agreements
  • Government requests or legal proceedings
  • Fraud prevention and safety concerns
User Controls

Users can edit account information and preferences. MPM retains the right to send service announcements and administrative communications.

Security

MPM implements physical and electronic safeguards and limits employee access to necessary personnel only.

Contact

For privacy-related queries write to: legal@mpminfosoft.com
